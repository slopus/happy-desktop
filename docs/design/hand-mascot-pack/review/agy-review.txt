### Detailed Clip Evaluations

#### 1. `01-finger-heart-motion` — **NEEDS FIX**
* **Choreography Collapse (0.75s – 1.50s):** The hand abandons the finger heart gesture and flattens out into an open 5-finger splayed palm / wave.
* **Detached Prop (0.75s – 1.50s):** During the open-palm phase, the pink heart floats detached in empty space beside the thumb.
* **Flicker / Snapping (1.75s):** The hand pops abruptly back from the open palm into the finger heart gesture without a smooth transition.

#### 2. `02-wave-motion` — **NEEDS FIX**
* **Thumb Mutation & Severe Swelling (1.75s – 2.25s):** At 1.75s the thumb curls forward into a bulbous knob; at 2.00s it elongates into a foot-like appendage; at 2.25s it grotesquely swells into a massive, muscular horizontal arm / bicep shape extending off the side.
* **Morph Snapping (2.50s):** Abruptly shrinks back into a stubby hand before recovering at 2.75s.

#### 3. `03-crossed-fingers-motion` — **NEEDS FIX**
* **Surface / Face Identity Swap (0.00s – 2.75s):** The palm side displays a downturned frowny mouth instead of the fixed smiling palm required by the mascot identity spec.
* **Violent Uncrossing Flicker (1.00s – 2.25s):** Toggles violently between crossed fingers and an uncrossed "peace sign" every frame (uncrossed at 1.00s, crossed at 1.25s, uncrossed at 1.50s, crossed at 1.75s, uncrossed at 2.00s, crossed at 2.25s).
* **Thumb Elongation (1.00s, 1.50s):** Thumb stretches straight up into an unnatural third tall vertical finger.
* **Melting / Smearing (2.00s):** Severe ghosting blur and melting artifact between the two extended finger tips.

#### 4. `04-thumbs-up-motion` — **NEEDS FIX**
* **Missing Thumb (0.25s):** Thumb retracts completely into the fist, leaving a thumbless 4-knuckle ball.
* **Choreography Breakdown (0.75s – 1.00s & 2.25s – 2.50s):** Rather than maintaining a thumbs-up pose, the hand twice violently bursts open into a wide 5-finger splayed palm.
* **Unstable Hand Identity (1.25s, 2.75s):** Thumb points sideways like a hitchhiker rather than vertically upward.

#### 5. `05-holding-heart-motion` — **NEEDS FIX**
* **Digit Count Loss (2.00s – 2.50s):** Digit count drops from 5 to 4 (only 3 upper/side fingers plus thumb are visible; the fifth digit vanishes or merges into the palm).
* **Face Occlusion / Melting (2.25s – 2.50s):** The heart slides down too far, completely covering the mascot's mouth and partially submerging the right eye.

#### 6. `06-beckoning-motion` — **NEEDS FIX**
* **Phantom Digit / Split Glitch (0.75s):** The articulating finger splits/deforms into two simultaneous digits: an upright vertical digit on the far right and a separate horizontal finger curling flat across the mascot's forehead/eyes.
* **Facial Expression Degradation (2.75s):** The smiling mouth degrades into an expressionless flat dash (`-`).

#### 7. `07-finger-dance-motion` — **PASS**
* **Choreography & Inversion:** Accurately flips upside down by 0.75s with the wrist cleanly positioned at the **TOP**.
* **Walking Steps:** Accurately lands on index and middle fingertips and executes continuous alternating walking steps across 1.00s – 2.75s.
* **Digit Integrity:** Remaining digits stay folded (thumb extended outward for balance, ring/pinky folded). Strict 5-digit count maintained throughout; no extra limbs, feet, or digits.
* **Face Orientation:** The smiling face remains physically anchored to the palm, rotating upside-down naturally with the hand geometry.

#### 8. `08-turnaround-back-motion` — **NEEDS FIX**
* **Missing Face Texture (0.75s – 1.75s):** When the hand completes its first 180° rotation to reveal the back surface, the back is completely blank yellow with no frowny face.
* **Erratic Spin & Popping (2.00s – 2.50s):** The hand executes an unprompted second rotation, and the frowny face abruptly pops onto the back at 2.50s.

#### 9. `09-back-stop-motion` — **NEEDS FIX**
* **Facial Feature Erasure (1.25s – 2.75s):** After clenching into a fist at 0.50s and opening back up, the frowny mouth completely disappears. For more than half the clip (1.25s through 2.75s), the mascot has only two blank dot eyes (`. .`) and no mouth.

#### 10. `10-back-middle-finger-motion` — **PASS**

* **Digit Count & Shape:** Exactly 5 digits maintained throughout (2 folded on left, middle extended, 2 folded on right). No thumb swelling, melting, or anatomical distortions.
* **Animation:** Rhythmic side-to-side wagging of the extended finger with clean, synchronized squint/blink keyframes (1.00s – 1.25s, 2.25s).

### Limits of Sampling Notice
These assessments are based on temporal contact sheets sampled at **4 fps (250 ms intervals)** across each 3.0-second video. Brief sub-frame glitches, intra-frame jitter/micro-flicker, or lighting strobing occurring strictly between sampled 250 ms intervals cannot be detected from static temporal grids alone.
